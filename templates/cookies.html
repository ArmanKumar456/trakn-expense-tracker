Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2024

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit TRAKN. They help us remember your preferences and keep you signed in.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for the app to function (login session, theme preference)
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how the app is used so we can improve it
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings like dark/light mode

3. How Long Cookies Last

  • Session cookies expire when you close the browser
  • Persistent cookies last up to 12 months
  • You can clear cookies at any time via your browser settings

4. Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Any third-party services we use are bound by strict data protection agreements.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect app functionality. To opt out of analytics cookies, contact us at support@trakn.com.
